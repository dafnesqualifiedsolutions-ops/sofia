06 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Sofia works with your existing business number. We redirect calls to her behind the scenes — your customers dial the same number they always have.

What languages does Sofia speak?

Sofia speaks English, Spanish, French, German, Dutch, Italian, Portuguese, and more. She detects the caller's language automatically and responds in it.

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

Sofia is clear that she's an AI assistant. Most callers are happy with fast, accurate answers — and if they ask for a human, she can take a message or transfer the call.

What happens if Sofia doesn't understand?

She'll ask the caller to repeat or rephrase. If she still can't help, she takes a detailed message and sends it to you instantly — you never miss the lead.

Can Sofia book appointments into my calendar?

Yes — we can connect Sofia to Google Calendar, Outlook, or most booking systems. Appointments appear in real time.

Do I need technical skills to set this up?

Not at all. We handle the entire setup. You just tell us your hours, services, and any specific questions you get asked. We do the rest.

What's the cancellation policy?

Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are non-refundable but can be cancelled before renewal.

Is this GDPR compliant?

Yes. All call data is stored securely in the UK. We only collect what's necessary to handle the call and pass leads to you. You can request full deletion at any time.
